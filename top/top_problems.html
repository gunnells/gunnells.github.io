Math 671 Problems

Problems are taken from Lee, Introduction to Topological Manifolds, 2nd ed. unless indicated otherwise. Exercises, denoted with an E, appear in-line in the chapter text and use a period for punctuation. Problems, denoted with a P, appear at the end of each chapter and use a hyphen for punctuation. Thus E2.2 appears on p.21 immediately after Example 2.1. P2–1 is the first problem of Chapter 2, which appears on p.45. Problems from Hatcher appear at the end of Chapter 0 and at the end of subsections within other chapters.
